# NieR: Automata — single-game artwork test

Compared official candidates using game identity, composition, centered 16:9 crop compatibility and technical quality. Downloaded originals decode with Pillow; visually inspected originals and a temporary Chromium crop-review sheet at 960 × 540 per image. The single-game title sits above the hero, so no rankings-style text overlay was applied. No infographic was generated. Research JSON, schemas and historical images/sidecars were not modified. This was a targeted candidate comparison, not an exhaustive search of all published artwork.
